Under the Spell of Cryptos? Crypto-Equity Spillovers and the Role of Market Integration

🏆 FIBA 2026 — Best PhD Paper in Finance

This paper investigates time-varying volatility spillovers between crypto-assets and equity markets. Using a DCC-GARCH framework across 20 emerging and developed markets, we document that crypto-equity integration amplifies contagion during stress episodes and that market integration is the key moderator of spillover intensity.

SSRN FIBA Award

Crypto-Assets Financial Instability: A Tale of Two Tails

Financial Instability · Financial Fragility · Quantile Regression

We develop a rolling quantile-regression framework to classify crypto-assets monthly as Financially Instable (FI), Financially Fragile (FF), or Stable. We then back-test equally-weighted portfolios sorted on this classification and show that FI/FF timing generates significant out-of-sample alpha relative to a Top-10 benchmark.

Do CBDCs Crowd Out Stablecoins? Evidence from Central Bank Communication

with Theo Lamagnère · NLP · BIS Speeches Corpus · Jev & BERT classifiers

Using a novel corpus of 635 BIS central bank speeches (2018–2026), we apply LLM-based sentiment classification (Jev model) to construct monthly CBDC stance and sentiment indices. We test whether stronger pro-CBDC communication precedes a decline in stablecoin market share.
